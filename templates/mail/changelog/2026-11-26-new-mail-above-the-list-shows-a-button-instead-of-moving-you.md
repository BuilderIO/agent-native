When new mail arrives above the part of the list you're reading, Mail keeps your place and shows a "3 new" button at the top. Click it to go to the new mail, or press Home to go to the top of the list. With the reading pane on and nothing open, the pane names the marked email's sender and subject and says J or Enter opens it.
