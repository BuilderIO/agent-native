Unread and Starred are now filters: the Filter button beside the sort menu narrows any inbox tab, or All Mail, to unread or starred mail, and the filtered list is a link you can share or bookmark. The old Unread page and Shift+U open the inbox filtered by Unread; the old Starred page, G S, and Go to Starred open All Mail filtered by Starred, so starred mail you archived stays in view.
