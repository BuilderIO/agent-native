The exact installed license is retained in this app's public distribution at `public/licenses/mediabunny-1.61.3-LICENSE.txt` (SHA-256 `3f3d9e0024b1921b067d6f7f88deb4a60cbe7a78e76c64e3f1d7fc3b779b9d04`).

Upstream source: <https://github.com/Vanilagy/mediabunny>. Package: <https://www.npmjs.com/package/mediabunny/v/1.61.3>. This integration does not modify Mediabunny's source. The local MP4 output consists of the user's rendered Design frames; it does not contain Mediabunny source code.
