# Appointment Blocker Design

- Mode: `operate`
- Audience: people protecting work time around personal appointments
- Direction: Cobalt Workbench
- Palette family: Cobalt / sky
- Type: Sans-first, compact operational hierarchy
- Composition: Focused three-step workbench with one decision per state
- Shape language: Crisp borders, quiet cards, no decorative panels
- Anti-references: Terracotta default, card grids, status-chip soup, generic
  AI decoration
