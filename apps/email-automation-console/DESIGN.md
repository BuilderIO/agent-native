# Email Automation Console Design

- Mode: `operate`
- Audience: operators checking and improving recurring email runs
- Direction: Pine Control Room
- Palette family: Pine / teal
- Type: Sans-first, scannable utility hierarchy
- Composition: Sparse control room with a focused preview and contextual rail
- Shape language: Soft utility surfaces with restrained borders
- Anti-references: Terracotta default, marketing dashboard density, equal-weight
  cards, generic AI decoration
