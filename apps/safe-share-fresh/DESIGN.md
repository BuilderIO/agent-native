# Safe Share Design

- Mode: `read`
- Audience: people reviewing sensitive transcripts before sharing them
- Direction: Paper and Ink
- Palette family: Paper / charcoal
- Type: Sans-first review UI with legible document text
- Composition: Original and safe result stacked vertically with setup revealed
  beside the active review
- Shape language: Tactile paper surfaces, dark action, quiet boundaries
- Anti-references: Terracotta default, dramatic color, side-by-side long-form
  comparison, generic AI decoration
