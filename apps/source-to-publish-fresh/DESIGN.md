# Source to Publish Design

- Mode: `read`
- Audience: people turning source material into a publishable draft
- Direction: Editorial Berry
- Palette family: Plum / berry, used as a restrained publishing accent
- Type: Sans-first shell with editorial contrast inside the draft preview
- Composition: Document workbench with source, format, draft, and agent review
- Shape language: Paper surfaces, quiet borders, generous reading measure
- Anti-references: Warm palette as a workspace default, dense dashboards,
  side-by-side long-form comparison, generic AI decoration
