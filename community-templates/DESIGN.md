# Community templates visual direction

## Shadcn operations workspace

- **Mode:** focused, dense operations tools for testing one repeatable GTM job
  at a time.
- **Audience:** RevOps, marketing ops, sales, and recruiting operators who need
  a useful cloneable starting point, not a product tour.
- **Visual world:** the same light shadcn surface language used by Design,
  Slides, and Clips: white canvas, neutral sidebar, thin borders, black primary
  actions, and semantic accent colors only for state.
- **Type:** Inter Variable with compact labels, clear 14-26px headings, and
  data-dense rows instead of explanatory paragraphs.
- **Composition:** every app owns one route and one job. A small app sidebar
  orients the user, the main pane carries the workflow, and the persistent
  Agent Sidebar stays available for judgment and write-back planning.
- **Shape:** 6-8px control and surface radii, 1px semantic borders, subtle
  surface shadows, and short ease-out transitions.
- **Anti-references:** no dark dashboard chrome, oversized hero copy, generic
  template rail, decorative AI symbols, nested cards, or status-chip soup.
