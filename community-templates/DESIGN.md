# Community templates visual direction

## Signal room

- **Mode:** dense operations console for people testing repeatable GTM work.
- **Audience:** RevOps, marketing ops, sales, and recruiting operators.
- **Visual world:** graphite workspace chrome with paper-like content surfaces,
  amber emphasis for attention, and muted teal for completed agent work.
- **Type:** compact sans-serif hierarchy with tabular data set tight enough to
  scan, while the selected record gets generous reading space.
- **Composition:** left template rail, central workflow surface, contextual
  agent plan on the right. Each route shows one job and one next action.
- **Shape:** lightly rounded rows and panels, 1px rules, restrained shadows,
  and short state transitions that clarify what changed.
- **Anti-references:** no gradient hero, sparkle AI iconography, decorative
  dashboard cards, or explanatory feature tour in the working surface.
