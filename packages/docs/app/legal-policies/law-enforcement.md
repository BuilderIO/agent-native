# Law Enforcement Request Policy

Updated September 2, 2026

> This standalone Agent-Native policy is based on Builder.io's policy revision dated August 19, 2026. It applies only to hosted Agent-Native services operated by Builder.io. The MIT-licensed framework, forks, and self-hosted deployments are outside its scope. The English version controls.

This policy is informational. It does not create rights for any party or waive any objection Builder may raise.

This policy explains how Builder.io, Inc. ("Builder") responds to requests from law enforcement and other government authorities for hosted application user or end-user information. Builder is committed to protecting its users' data and discloses information only when legally required. This policy is informational and does not create rights for any party or waive any objection Builder may raise.

1. Valid Legal Process Required

1.1. Builder discloses hosted application user or end-user information only in response to valid, enforceable legal process appropriate to the type of information sought  -  for example, a subpoena for basic subscriber information, a court order for certain non-content records, or a warrant for content. Builder reviews each request for validity, scope, and authority, and will narrow or object to requests that are overbroad or legally deficient. Where a request concerns personal data protected by the EU or UK General Data Protection Regulation ("GDPR") or similar non-US comprehensive data protection law, Builder also assesses the request for compatibility with applicable data protection law. Valid legal process in the requesting jurisdiction is not by itself a lawful basis for disclosure of such data.

1.2. Requests must be submitted by an official government or law-enforcement email domain to legal@builder.io and must identify the requesting authority, the legal authority for the request, the specific records sought, and the relevant time period.

2. Requests from Non-U.S. Authorities

Builder responds to requests from government authorities outside the United States through applicable international legal cooperation mechanisms, such as a mutual legal assistance treaty (MLAT), and may direct requesting authorities to those channels.

Where a request seeks personal data protected by GDPR, Builder will assess the request under applicable data protection law, including the rules governing international data transfers, and will act consistently with its data processing agreements and any applicable standard contractual clauses.

3. Notice to Users

Builder's policy is to notify the affected user of a request for their information and to provide a reasonable opportunity to object before disclosing, unless prohibited by law, court order, or where Builder believes in good faith there is a risk of imminent harm.

Where a request is accompanied by a valid nondisclosure or gag order issued by a court, Builder will withhold notice for the period per the terms of the court order and will notify the user when permitted by such order.

4. Hosted and End-User Data

All data processed through Hosted Agent-Native Applications belongs to and is controlled by our users. Where appropriate and lawful, Builder will redirect requests for such data to the relevant user rather than producing it directly.

5. Emergency Requests

In emergencies involving a risk of death or serious physical harm, Builder may disclose limited information reasonably necessary to law enforcement without legal process where it believes in good faith that disclosure is necessary to prevent the harm. Emergency requests must come through legal@builder.io and describe the nature of the emergency.

6. What We Can Provide

The information available depends on the account and Service used, and may include basic account information such as name and email and, subject to appropriate legal process, certain logs or content. Builder cannot provide information it does not retain.

7. Cost Reimbursement

Builder may seek reimbursement for the reasonable costs of responding to requests, where permitted by law or pursuant to the terms of the applicable written agreement.
