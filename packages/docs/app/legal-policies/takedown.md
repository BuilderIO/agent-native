# Suspension, Takedown & Data-Handling Policy

Updated September 2, 2026

> This standalone Agent-Native policy applies only to hosted Agent-Native services operated under the Agent-Native name. The MIT-licensed framework, forks, and self-hosted deployments are outside its scope. The English version controls.

The retrieval period is an operational commitment for the hosted examples. It does not apply where preservation or disclosure is prohibited by law or would create a safety or legal risk.

This policy explains how and when the Service operator suspends access to Agent-Native hosted services, takes down Hosted Agent-Native Applications, or otherwise restricts access, how users can appeal, and what happens to data after a suspension or takedown. It supplements the Agent-Native Hosted Services Terms, the Acceptable Use Policy, and the Platform Rules.

1. Grounds for Action

The Service operator may suspend an account, restrict access to the Services, or disable a Hosted Agent-Native Application where it reasonably believes there is:

- a violation of the Acceptable Use Policy or Platform Rules;
- a credible copyright or other intellectual-property infringement claim submitted through the reporting or contact method published on the Agent-Native Site, including repeat infringement;
- a security threat, such as malware, phishing, or compromised infrastructure;
- unlawful content or activity, or a valid legal order requiring removal;
- unlicensed operation of a regulated or age-restricted offering.

2. Notice

2.1 Standard Notice. Where practical and not prohibited by law or an urgent need to protect the Services or others, the Service operator will notify the affected user of a suspension or takedown, including the reason and steps required to restore service.

2.2 Immediate Action. For urgent threats - including active malware, phishing, CSAM, or a credible security or legal emergency - the Service operator may act immediately and provide notice afterward.

3. Tiered Response

The Service operator aims to use the least disruptive effective measure:

- content-level removal or disabling of the specific offending material;
- application-level suspension of an individual Hosted Agent-Native Application;
- account-level suspension where violations are severe, repeated, or account-wide.

4. Appeals

4.1. Appeals should use the reporting or contact method published on the Agent-Native Site with the subject "Appeal," including the affected URL or project identifier and relevant context. Appeals will be acknowledged and escalated to the appropriate team for resolution.

5. Data Handling During Suspension and After Takedown

5.1 During Suspension. While a Hosted Agent-Native Application is suspended, hosted application data is generally retained and preserved, but access to it and to the application may be restricted.

5.2. Hosted Application Data Retrieval. Except where prohibited by law, where preservation is required for a legal hold, or where the grounds for action involve CSAM, active malware, fraud, or other serious violations where data preservation would itself be harmful or unlawful, the Service operator will provide the user at least 30 days from notice to export or retrieve hosted application data before deletion.

5.3 End-User Data. End-user data collected through a suspended or removed Hosted Agent-Native Application remains the user's responsibility as controller. The user is responsible for any notices to, or requests from, its end users arising from the suspension or takedown.

5.4 Deletion. Following termination resulting from a takedown, hosted application data is deleted in accordance with the Agent-Native Privacy Policy, except where retention is legally required.
