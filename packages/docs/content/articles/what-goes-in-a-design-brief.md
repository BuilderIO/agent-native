A design brief is a short document that says what you're making, who it's for, and the limits it has to work within. A good one fits on a page and answers the questions a designer, or an AI design tool, would otherwise have to guess at.

This guide is about briefs for websites, landing pages, and product screens. The template below works for most of them.

## What goes in a design brief?

| Section | What to write |
| --- | --- |
| Overview | What you're making and why, in two or three sentences. |
| Goal | The one outcome that tells you it worked. |
| Audience | Who it's for and what they already know. |
| Scope and deliverables | What's in, what's out, and what you expect to receive. |
| Brand and style | Colors, type, tone, and anything to match or avoid. |
| Constraints | Deadline, budget, technical limits, and required content. |
| Success measures | How you'll judge the result. |

## What does a design brief look like in practice?

Here's a short, made-up example for a landing page.

> Project: Landing page for a habit-tracking app.
> Overview: A one-page site that explains the app and gets visitors to join the waitlist.
> Goal: Waitlist signups.
> Audience: People who've tried habit apps and quit after a few weeks.
> Scope: Hero, three features, FAQ, signup form. No pricing page.
> Brand: Calm, plain language, one accent color.
> Constraints: Needs to ship Friday. Static HTML only.
> Success: Signup rate from visitors.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is a design brief?

A design brief says what you're making, who it's for, and the limits it has to work within.

### How long should a design brief be?

One page is usually enough. If it runs longer, the extra detail often belongs in a separate spec.

### Who writes a design brief?

Whoever owns the goal: a product manager, a marketer, a founder, or the designer after a kickoff conversation.

### Can I use AI to write a design brief?

Yes. You can ask an AI agent to draft one from your notes. Check it against what you actually need, because it fills gaps with guesses.

### Is Design free?

Yes. Design is free and open source. It's labeled alpha, so expect rough edges.
