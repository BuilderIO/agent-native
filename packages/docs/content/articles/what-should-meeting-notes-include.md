Meeting notes should record what was decided, who owns each next step and by when, and what's still open. A transcript isn't notes, and a summary of who said what isn't either. The template below sticks to those three things plus the basics.

The format matters less than whether anyone reads the notes next week. Short notes with named owners get read. Long ones don't.

## What goes in meeting notes?

| Section | What to write |
| --- | --- |
| Basics | Date, attendees, and the purpose of the meeting in one sentence. |
| Decisions | What was decided, as plain statements. |
| Action items | Who will do what, by when. |
| Open questions | What's unresolved, and who will find the answer. |
| Context | Anything a person who missed the meeting needs in order to understand the decisions. |

## What's a meeting notes template you can copy?

```
Meeting: [Name]
Date:
Attendees:
Purpose:

Decisions:
-

Action items:
- [Owner]: [What] by [Date]

Open questions:
- [Question] (owner: [Name])

Context for people who weren't there:
```

## Should you write the notes yourself or use an AI note-taker?

| Your situation | Option | What to check |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A small meeting where one person can write while listening | Write the notes by hand with the template | The writer is half out of the conversation. |
| A larger meeting, or you need to take part | Rotate the note-taker | Quality varies by writer, and owners and dates get dropped. |
| You want notes without taking them | An AI note-taker | Check names, numbers, and decisions against what was said, and check consent to record. |
| You want recordings, notes, and search in one library | Clips meetings | Connect Google Calendar, hit record on a meeting, and get a transcript, summary, notes, and action items. Check consent, and expect rough edges, since Clips is alpha. |

Agent-Native publishes Clips, so weigh the last row accordingly. Handwritten notes are the right call for a short meeting where you're the main speaker.

## What should you check before you record a meeting?

Recording rules and company policies vary. Tell participants before you record, and check the rules that apply to you. For sensitive meetings, such as performance reviews or legal discussions, decide whether a recording is appropriate at all. AI summaries can miss or garble details, so read the notes against the transcript before you share them.

## Frequently asked questions

### What's the difference between meeting notes and meeting minutes?

Minutes are a formal record of what was decided, often kept for governance. Notes are a working record for the team. For most team meetings, notes are enough.

### How long should meeting notes be?

As short as they can be while still letting someone who missed the meeting act on them. Put decisions and action items first.

### Can AI take meeting notes?

Yes. AI note-takers transcribe and summarize meetings. They can miss or garble details, so check names, numbers, and decisions before you share the notes.

### Do I need permission to record a meeting?

Recording rules and company policies vary. Tell participants before you record, and check the rules that apply to you.
