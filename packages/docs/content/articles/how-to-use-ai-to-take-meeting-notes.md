Record the meeting and let an AI note-taker write the transcript, a summary, and the action items while you take part in the conversation. Then spend a couple of minutes checking names, numbers, and decisions before you share. That check is the part AI can't do for you.

## What does AI handle, and what still needs you?

| AI handles | You still own |
| --- | --- |
| Transcribing what was said | Telling participants you're recording |
| A first-pass summary | Checking names, numbers, and decisions against the transcript |
| Pulling out action items | Confirming each owner and date |
| Finding a past meeting later | Deciding when a meeting shouldn't be recorded at all |

## How do you choose an AI note-taker?

| What to check | Why it matters |
| --- | --- |
| Where the notes live | Notes you can't find again don't help anyone. |
| What it connects to | A calendar connection decides how much setup each meeting takes. |
| How you check the summary | You need the transcript next to the summary to verify it. |
| Who can see the recordings | Meetings often include things not everyone should hear. |

## What should the notes include?

Even with AI, good notes record decisions, owners and dates, and open questions. Use this template to check an AI summary, or to write notes by hand:

```
Meeting: [Name]
Date:
Attendees:
Purpose:

Decisions:
-

Action items:
- [Owner]: [What] by [Date]

Open questions:
- [Question] (owner: [Name])

Context for people who weren't there:
```

## What should you check before you record?

Recording rules and company policies vary. Tell participants before you record, and check the rules that apply to you. For sensitive meetings, like performance reviews or legal discussions, decide whether a recording is appropriate at all. AI summaries can miss or garble details, so read the notes against the transcript before you share them.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can AI take meeting notes?

Yes. AI note-takers transcribe meetings and draft a summary and action items. You still check names, numbers, and decisions before you share them.

### Are AI meeting notes accurate?

They can miss or garble details, especially names and numbers. Read the summary against the transcript before you share it.

### Do I need permission to record a meeting?

Recording rules and company policies vary. Tell participants before you record, and check the rules that apply to you.

### What's the difference between meeting notes and meeting minutes?

Minutes are a formal record of what was decided, often kept for governance. Notes are a working record for the team. For most team meetings, notes are enough.
