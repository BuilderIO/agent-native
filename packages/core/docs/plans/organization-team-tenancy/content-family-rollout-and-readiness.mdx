# Content family rollout and readiness

Parent: [organization-team tenancy implementation plan](./plan.mdx)

## Decision

Content is one independently enabled template family. Core completes first, then every template receives and locally proves its own complete tenancy contract. Content's team-scope flag remains off by default until its own migration and checks pass.

## Grounded seams

- `templates/content/server/db/schema.ts` owns Content durable records and additive schema changes.
- `templates/content/shared/api.ts` owns Content's shared API contract.
- `packages/core/src/sharing/access.ts` (`accessFilter`) and `packages/core/src/sharing/registry.ts` supply shared enforcement and family registration.
- `packages/core/src/resources/handlers.ts`, `packages/core/src/agent/run-manager.ts`, and `packages/core/src/jobs/background-automation-runner.ts` are required inventory seams for HTTP, durable-agent, and background paths.

## Ordered steps

1. Inventory every Content read, list, search, export, item, create/update/delete, share, legacy HTTP, agent, and background/automation path from `schema.ts` and `shared/api.ts` outward. Include transclusion, traversal, embeds, projections, caches, snippets, counts, pagination, search, export, AI, and background surfaces. Record which paths are family-owned and which reuse core enforcement.
2. Add Content's tenancy fields through an additive migration only after the shared scope contract exists. Preserve every pre-existing row's owner, effective scope, `orgId` association, visibility, ACL, and behavior. Do not map or reclassify rows implicitly.
3. Route Content API operations through trusted scope validation and `accessFilter`; make unknown, mismatched, or disabled team scope deny access and deny team creates/moves.
4. Define Content's explicit audited move path and share-compatibility checks using the shared move contract. Do not create Content-specific scope semantics.
5. Verify every inventoried Content surface locally with three-scope fixtures, revoked membership/grants, archived teams, and pre-existing rows.
6. Enable the Content flag only after its migration, family registration, complete inventory, and local proof are complete. Do not add a deployment or live-server readiness gate.

## Acceptance criteria

- Content remains disabled for team scope until its independent local contract passes.
- Pre-existing Content rows preserve their established owner, effective scope, organization association, visibility, ACL, and behavior. No broad backfill or implicit scope conversion occurs.
- Every Content surface uses trusted scope and shared enforcement, including transclusion, traversal, embeds, projections, caches, snippets, counts, pagination, search, export, AI, and background/agent flows.
- Missing registration, unknown scope, or a disabled flag denies team behavior rather than coercing it.

## Risks and non-goals

- Do not use Content as a shortcut to enable unrelated resource families.
- Do not treat Content as the only template that needs complete family proof.
- Do not add ownership semantics that differ from [shared scope, access, and moves](./shared-scope-access-and-moves.mdx).

## Dependencies

Depends on [governance and schema](./governance-and-schema.mdx), [shared scope, access, and moves](./shared-scope-access-and-moves.mdx), [connections, runs, and automations](./connections-runs-and-automations.mdx), and [UI selection and administration](./ui-selection-and-administration.mdx). Completion permits only the separate Content family-flag decision. Every other template requires the same independent local proof.
