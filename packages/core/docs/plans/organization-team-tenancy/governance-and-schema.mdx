# Governance and schema

Parent: [organization-team tenancy implementation plan](./plan.mdx)

## Decision

Add dedicated durable team and membership records in the organization layer. A team belongs to exactly one organization. Team-membership rows may be created only for active organization members; team-derived authority stops immediately when organization membership becomes inactive, including while a federation-removal row awaits cleanup. Users can belong to multiple organizations and retain one active-organization selection for existing list scoping. A user can join zero or more teams across organizations where the user is an active member. Organization owners/admins administer every team, including leadless teams. A team can have zero or more leads. A lead manages only members of their own team and cannot remove or demote another lead. Leads have no inherent resource or credential authority. Deletion becomes archival with a stable historical identifier.

## Grounded seams

- `packages/core/src/org/schema.ts` and `packages/core/src/org/types.ts` own organization membership schema and role types.
- `packages/core/src/org/handlers.ts` is the organization mutation boundary.
- `packages/core/src/org/context.ts` establishes organization context used by protected operations.
- `packages/core/src/client/org/TeamPage.tsx` is an existing organization administration surface to extend rather than replace.

## Ordered steps

1. Add only additive team and team-membership records in `org/schema.ts`; require one organization per team and active membership in that organization when creating a team-membership row. Model the member identity, `lead`/`member` role, archival state, and stable ID without altering multi-organization membership or active-organization selection semantics.
2. Extend `org/types.ts` with the team role and validated team-context types. Keep organization role authority distinct from team role authority.
3. Implement organization-handler operations for create, membership change, leave, offboarding coordination, and archival in `org/handlers.ts`. Enforce organization membership, team-local lead authority, and atomic removal of team-derived memberships. Do not require a replacement lead.
4. Extend `org/context.ts` only with server-derived team lookup/validation primitives. An absent, foreign, or archived team must produce no usable team authority.
5. Make archival show live team-resource, context, automation, and grant blockers. Resolve eligible authored resources only through authorized audited moves. Stop or reassign automations, revoke grants, remove team context and learning, retain or remove immovable team-bound records by policy, and atomically recheck before retaining the team ID/history as archived. Inherited organization resources are not blockers.
6. Add focused tests for cross-team lead denial, leadless-team governance, lead leave/removal, owner/admin override, organization-member removal, transient federation-removal team rows with no team-derived authority, archival blockers, atomic recheck, and team-derived authority/link revocation.

## Acceptance criteria

- A team belongs to exactly one organization. Team-membership rows are created only for active organization members; a retained row during offboarding or `federationRemovalPendingAt` cleanup never implies team-derived authority.
- Users can belong to multiple organizations and retain one active-organization selection for existing list scoping. They can join zero or more teams across organizations where they are active members.
- A lead cannot administer another team or remove/demote another lead solely through the lead role.
- Lead leave, removal, and organization offboarding work without replacement leads.
- Archival preserves the stable team ID and history while removing live team authority. It is blocked until live team resources, context, automations, and grants are resolved. It does not delete inherited organization resources.
- Existing organizations without teams preserve their current behavior.

## Risks and non-goals

- Do not reuse scheduling-domain `team_members` or generic sharing groups as tenancy records.
- Do not turn team membership into organization membership, resource ownership, or an ACL override.
- Do not add resource-family scope columns here; that belongs to [shared scope, access, and moves](./shared-scope-access-and-moves.mdx).

Requires the ADR decision only. Blocks [shared scope, access, and moves](./shared-scope-access-and-moves.mdx), [connections, runs, and automations](./connections-runs-and-automations.mdx), and [UI selection and administration](./ui-selection-and-administration.mdx).
