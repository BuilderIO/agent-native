Bundle a `fusion-starter` scaffold template: a blank, English-only app canvas with Drizzle wiring that `agent-native template materialize --template fusion-starter` produces directly. It ships only the skills it changes and inherits the rest of the Chat starter skills, and `skills update scaffold` keeps those overrides for apps marked `scaffold.template: "fusion-starter"`.
