Scripts on the MCP script catalog can now call actions that change data, once an app turns on `AGENT_NATIVE_MCP_SCRIPT_WRITES`. An action opts in with `mcpScript: "write"` and an `mcpScriptWrite` contract in `defineAction`: an upper bound on the rows one call can change, the receipt it commits with its change, and a `reconcile` function that says whether that receipt exists. Before a write runs, its rows are reserved against the script's budget (`AGENT_NATIVE_MCP_SCRIPT_WRITE_ROW_BUDGET`, 200 by default) and a row is written to a new script ledger; a write whose ledger row cannot be written does not run. Each write is then settled as committed, not applied, or unknown, and a recurring sweep settles the unclear ones from their receipts, or as not applied once they could no longer commit (`AGENT_NATIVE_MCP_SCRIPT_WRITE_RECOVERY_MS`). Audit rows and change markers for committed writes are delivered through the ledger's outbox and retried until they land. A new `get-script-run` tool shows a run's writes and refused calls to the person who ran it or an admin of their organization, and `run-script` is no longer marked read-only where writes are on.
