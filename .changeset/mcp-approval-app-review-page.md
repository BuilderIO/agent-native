Show MCP approval reviews inside the app, in its own theme and components. When a signed-in reader opens a review link, an app whose agent chat mounts MCP (`appApprovalPage: true` on `mountMCP`) now sends them to its home page, with the grant id in the URL fragment (`#mcpApproval=<id>`). The fragment survives the redirects that apps' home routes often make. `AppProviders` then shows the review in place of the app: who the call reaches, every argument, times in the reader's time zone, the client that asked and when it was connected, and a warning when the identical call already ran. It leads with who asks and for what ("Claude Code wants to create event"), and after a decision it keeps one line naming the request. Email addresses wrap only after their "@", never inside a name. Approve and Deny go through the same framework route and token as before. The review is shown only in a top-level window, never under `sessionBypass`, on a public page or in another app's document. It sits outside the phone hand-off and first-run onboarding. Sign-in still happens at the link itself, so the link works after sign-in. A standalone `mountMCP` keeps the framework's plain page. In an app whose custom authentication has no `getBrowserSession` hook, the review says the app can't confirm the reader's sign-in instead of asking them to sign in, and the route's `401` carries `reason: "sign-in-unavailable"` to tell the two apart. The review's JSON now includes `locale`, and `@agent-native/core/client/mcp-approval` exports the browser helpers that read and decide a review. On every approval surface (this page, the plain page and the in-client form), a field the summary already shows is left out of the field list but kept in the details: a recipient list that holds only addresses, and a time zone that a time in the summary already shows. A value the schema lists as a choice reads in words (its `oneOf` or `anyOf` title, or else the enum value split into words, so `externalOnly` reads "External only"), and a time zone field reads by name ("Pacific Time"). A time whose zone shares the reader's offset but is another zone, such as London and Lagos in October, also shows the call's own time, naming its zone in full ("United Kingdom Time"), since that zone's own row is left out. `mountMCP` refuses `appApprovalPage` with a route prefix other than `/_agent-native`, the only one the app's review reads.
