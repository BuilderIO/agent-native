Search can find pages by meaning as well as by words. A search index registered with `semantic: true` and an `audience` keeps page-chunk embeddings fresh through a second change-feed consumer, `search-embeddings`, which an app adds with `semanticSearchMigration()`. `searchSemanticIndex()` returns the nearest pages the searcher's audiences may match, or says why it couldn't (`off`, or `unavailable` with `no-provider`, `no-vector-store` or `error`), and never throws. It drops matches below the model's `minSimilarity`, a new optional `EmbeddingFamily` field: 0.30 for Builder's model and voyage-multimodal-3.5, so a question no page answers finds nothing by meaning. Embeddings use the organization's embedding provider and record usage for every call. Meaning search is on by default; an organization admin can turn it off in Settings → Infrastructure or with `manage-service-providers` `{ semanticSearch: false }`, which deletes the organization's vectors and those of its members' pages with no organization, which its provider embeds, and turning it back on queues every page again. Vectors live in pgvector by default; `configureSemanticSearch({ vectorStore })` swaps the store, and `createMemorySearchVectorStore()` from `@agent-native/core/testing` runs the whole path in tests without pgvector.
