Settings → Appearance now has a Theme row in every app, with System, Light and Dark, so people can see and change whether an app follows their computer. The early head script and the running app now keep the same saved theme, and a theme sent by a host app (the desktop app, an embedding page) shows without being saved as the person's own choice.
