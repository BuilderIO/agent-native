The remix skill now finishes every remix on a Design board: the original, the remix, and a row of small "How it works" explainer frames whose knobs are the same Design tweaks as the remix, so turning one updates both. Each explainer is an exploded view of one step of the remix screen: its real layers as thin planes pulled apart in depth (dashed outlines for clip and empty layers, labels beside each plane, the composited result in front), with an explode toggle, hover highlight, drag tilt, a small paper-style panel of the step's real knobs and at most one handwritten note, its readout and motion read from those shared knobs; each part a knob drives is its own selectable layer that shows just that knob. The board is annotated on the canvas in an editorial style: paper regions with hairlines, section titles with serif-italic indices, margin notes and thin connectors. Saying no Design, headless, or just the code keeps the image-based repo finish.
