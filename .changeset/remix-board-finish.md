The remix skill now finishes every remix on a Design board: the original, the remix, and a row of small "How it works" explainer frames whose knobs are the same Design tweaks as the remix, so turning one updates both. Each explainer is a small isometric machine whose moving parts act out that stage, with its readout and motion read from those shared knobs. The board is annotated on the canvas in an editorial style: paper regions with hairlines, section titles with serif-italic indices, margin notes and thin connectors. Saying no Design, headless, or just the code keeps the image-based repo finish.
