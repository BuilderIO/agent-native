The remix skill now finishes every remix on a Design board: the original, the remix, and a row of small "How it works" explainer frames whose knobs are the same Design tweaks as the remix, so turning one updates both. Each explainer draws one step of how the screen is produced as flat, front-on 2D layers of the actual screen (offset panes for page, effect and background, one accent tracked across the row, margin sliders wired by hairlines), with its readout and motion read from those shared knobs; each part a knob drives is its own selectable layer that shows just that knob. The board is annotated on the canvas in an editorial style: paper regions with hairlines, section titles with serif-italic indices, margin notes and thin connectors. Saying no Design, headless, or just the code keeps the image-based repo finish.
