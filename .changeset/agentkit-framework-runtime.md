Introduce four headless AgentKit packages: a versioned provider-neutral protocol,
a deterministic conversation client, resumable HTTP and SSE adapters, and
executable transport conformance. Support validated events, replay and recovery,
cancellation, approvals, connection-request contracts, durable queued messages,
and follow-up promotion without overlapping runs.
