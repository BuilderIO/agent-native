## 1. Goal

The two impact steps of an investigation. "Watch replays" samples replays of
the affected people after the change started, finds the moment each one got
stuck, and says how many show the same stall ("34 show the same stall: the
table list stays on Loading tables"). It keeps 3 to 5 replay cards, each a
recording id and an offset that open Sessions at that moment. "Find affected
accounts" maps the affected people through the account map to accounts and
the MRR at risk ("23 accounts with $18,420 MRR"), and lists the people it
could not match. Replays can explain the change (one of 8.1a's four cause
checks); accounts never count toward confidence because they describe who is
hit, not why.

## 2. What the code does today

- `list-session-recordings` filters by one `userId`, `from`, `to`,
  `hasErrors`, friction signals and sorts
  (`actions/list-session-recordings.ts:46-166`); friction and speed filters
  and sorts need the Sessions triage Lab (`:172-188`). The library filter
  type is `SessionReplayListFilters` (`server/lib/session-replay.ts:126`) and
  the paged reader `listSessionRecordingsPage` (`:2557`). There is no filter
  for a set of people; 4.3 (brief step 4) adds a funnel-step filter.
- `get-session-replay-summary` adds `friction` when the Lab is on, where a
  null part means not measured, not zero, and returns `frictionError` or
  `labStateError` when a read fails (`actions/get-session-replay-summary.ts:27-80`).
  The friction shape and signals are in `shared/session-friction.ts:13-36`
  and `:191-235`; `isSessionsTriageLabEnabled` is
  `server/lib/sessions-triage-lab.ts:6`.
- `get-session-replay-timeline` returns a bounded, sanitized marker list
  (navigation, clicks, inputs, custom events, console errors, failed
  requests) and never raw DOM, inputs or chunks
  (`actions/get-session-replay-timeline.ts`, description and `eventLimit`).
- Replay deep links are `/sessions/<id>?atMs=<offset>`
  (`server/lib/session-replay.ts:2846-2854`), read by
  `app/pages/sessions/SessionDetailPage.tsx:307`.
- `matchErrorIssuesBySignatures` resolves console error lines to error
  issue ids with the ingest fingerprint (`actions/match-error-issues.ts:16-62`,
  `server/lib/error-capture.ts:1342`).
- Account map links: `analytics_account_links` with `provider` (`stripe`,
  `hubspot`, `product`), `external_id`, `email`, `domain`, `decision`, and
  an index on `(scope_key, email)` (`server/db/schema.ts:1008-1052`). Product
  users' ids are emails (`server/lib/account-map-sync.ts:50`). Readers go
  from accounts to people (`readAccountProductUsers`,
  `server/lib/revenue-feature-usage.ts:715`) and from Stripe customers to
  accounts (`readStripeAccountLinks`, `:687`); none goes from people to
  accounts. `resolveAccount` reports `map_empty` versus `not_found`
  (`server/lib/account-map-store.ts:629-643`).
- MRR per Stripe customer comes from 3.1b's revenue program customers view
  (`customer_id`, `currency`, `movement`, `mrr_window_start`,
  `mrr_current`; validated at `server/lib/revenue-feature-usage.ts:270-300`),
  run through `defaultRevenueFeatureUsageDeps().runRevenueProgram`
  (`:783-800`) after `findStripeRevenueProgramId` (`:760`). MRR only sums in
  one currency (`:307`). There is no plan field. 3.3c's
  `server/lib/account-stripe-billing.ts` (landed in `ec35c12590`) reads one
  account's Stripe billing at a time (`AccountStripeBillingResult`, `:143`);
  it fits a deep dive, not 500 people, so this unit uses the program.
- 8.1b (brief) provides `resolveAffectedPeople` (people `{ key, userId }`,
  500 cap, `truncated`) and the errors finding with its issue id.

## Plan corrections

- **No plan column.** The prototype's accounts table has a Plan column, but
  the revenue program's customers view has no plan. v1 shows Account, MRR and
  People stuck; Plan is left out rather than shown blank.
- **Replay cards carry no email.** The notebook can be shared with people who
  cannot open those replays, so the cell stores recording ids and offsets;
  8.1d reads each card's person label through the access-checked replay
  summary.

## 3. Build

1. **Session filter** (`server/lib/session-replay.ts`): add `userKeys?:
   string[]` (at most 500) to `SessionReplayListFilters`, matching the
   recording's `user_key`. It is a server-only filter and stays out of the
   `list-session-recordings` schema.
2. **Replays** `server/lib/investigation-checks/replays.ts`:
   - People: `resolveAffectedPeople` (8.1b). None, or a subject that is not
     person-based: `not_applicable` "No affected people to look for, so
     there are no replays to watch."
   - List recordings of those people that started after the onset (or the
     window start), `hideEmpty: true`, sorted by friction when the Lab is on,
     else by errors then newest. Sample 40 (standard budget), 80 (extended),
     or 10 when segments, releases and errors already explain the change
     (the prototype's "stopping early if they agree").
   - For each sampled recording, read the timeline with `eventLimit: 2000`,
     four at a time, each through the `meter`. The stall is the first of: a
     console error (resolved to an issue id with
     `matchErrorIssuesBySignatures`), a failed request (method, path pattern,
     status), a cluster of 3 or more dead or rage clicks on one target, or
     the last page before 20 seconds idle and then exit. The signature is the
     stall's kind plus its issue id, request pattern, target or path.
   - Group by signature. The dominant group explains the change when it holds
     at least half the sample and at least 5 recordings; `causeKey` is
     `error:<source>:<id>` when the signature is the errors finding's issue
     (so 8.1a can see they agree), else `stall:<signature>`.
   - Finding (`kind: "replays"`): title from a template ("People stall on
     <page or error> after <action>", or "Replays show no common stall"),
     `data = { sampled, withReplay, dominant: { count, signature, label },
     cards: [{ recordingId, atMs, durationMs, markerKind, markerLabel,
     accountId | null, rageClicks | null }] (3 to 5, from the dominant group,
     else the highest friction) }`. `markerLabel` is code-written from the
     marker ("Error SchemaSyncTimeoutError on /data-sources/postgres");
     8.1a's `finish-investigation` `replayCaptions` add the agent's one-line
     caption per card. `rageClicks` is null when friction was not measured.
   - Evidence: source `session-replay`, window, `rowCount` = sampled, filter
     "Friction scores need the Sessions triage Lab" when the Lab is off.
   - Couldn't check: `no_data_in_window` "None of the <N> affected people
     have a replay after the change started."; a timeline read that fails is
     counted and named in the evidence ("2 replays couldn't be read"), never
     dropped from the count.
3. **People to accounts** (`server/lib/account-map-store.ts`): add
   `readAccountsForProductUsers(scope, emails)` using the `(scope_key,
   email)` index, product links only, rejected links excluded, in chunks of
   1,000. Returns `{ byEmail: Map<email, accountId>, accounts: Map<id, {
   name }> }`.
4. **Accounts** `server/lib/investigation-checks/accounts.ts`:
   - People with a `userId` email go through `readAccountsForProductUsers`;
     anonymous people and unmatched emails are counted separately.
   - MRR: run the Stripe revenue program's customers view (`months: 1`)
     through `defaultRevenueFeatureUsageDeps`, map Stripe customers to
     accounts with `readStripeAccountLinks`, and sum `mrr_current` per account
     per currency. Mixed currencies list a total per currency and never add
     them together.
   - Finding (`kind: "accounts"`, `explains: null`): "<N> accounts and
     <MRR> MRR are affected", `data = { accounts: [{ accountId, name,
     mrr: { amountCents, currency } | null, people }] (top 5 by MRR), rest: {
     accounts, mrr, people }, totals: { accounts, mrrByCurrency,
     peopleMatched, peopleTotal, anonymous, unmatched }, truncated }`.
   - Evidence: source `account-map`, `rowCount` = people matched, filter
     "Matched by email through the account map", and the program's `asOf`.
   - Couldn't check: `no_data_in_window` "The account map is empty." (fix:
     link `/accounts`) when `resolveAccount` would report `map_empty`; MRR
     not set up gives a done step whose MRR values are null and a second
     couldn't-check entry "MRR: The Stripe revenue program isn't set up."
     (fix: agent); unmatched people add "<N> people with no account: Their
     email domains don't match an account in the account map." (fix: link
     `/accounts`), as the prototype draws.
5. **Register** both checks in `server/lib/investigation-checks/index.ts`.
   8.1a's couldn't-check cell gains these entries without change, since they
   are ordinary step results.
6. **Application state:** none.
7. **Skills:** extend `.agents/skills/investigations/SKILL.md`: what the
   replay and accounts steps read, that accounts never count toward
   confidence, and how to write replay captions (one line, what the person
   did and saw, from the marker, no guesses).
8. **Changelog:** `agent-native changelog add "Investigations now show replays of the people who got stuck and the accounts and MRR affected" --type added`.

## 8. Defaults for open questions

- [Default] Sample 40 replays, 80 on the extended budget, 10 once the cause
  checks agree; keep 3 to 5 cards.
- [Default] A stall group explains at half the sample and at least 5
  recordings.
- [Default] No Plan column in v1.
- [Default] Replay person labels are read at view time through the scoped
  summary, not stored.

## 9. Do not

- Do not store replay events, DOM, inputs, emails or timelines; ids, offsets,
  marker kinds and short labels only.
- Do not add MRR across currencies or show a missing MRR as $0.
- Do not count accounts toward confidence.
- No behavior changes outside the unit; additive-only schema; no new /api
  routes for app data (use actions); no raw fetch from client code; no
  process.env reads for provider credentials; no catch or default that turns a
  failure into a normal-looking value; no large payloads in SQL.
