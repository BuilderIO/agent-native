# 6.1b: Docs and docs-site pitch for operators

Lane B. Size M (copy is small; translating it into ten locales across two
surfaces is most of the work). Not gated. Soft dependency on 6.1a (shared
tagline), 6.2b (demo company step) and 2.1a (PostgreSQL claims).

Paths start at the repo root in this brief because nothing here is under
`templates/analytics/` except links back to it.

## 1. Goal

The Analytics docs page and the public Analytics page on the docs site speak
to founders, operators and growth teams without a data team: one answer
across Stripe, HubSpot and product data, kept as a dashboard, funnel or
alert, with a demo company to try first. They stop calling the app a
replacement for Amplitude, Mixpanel and Looker. Every locale of both surfaces
changes with the English source. All copy is a draft for the plan owner.
