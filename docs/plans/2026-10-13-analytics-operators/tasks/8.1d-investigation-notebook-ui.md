## 1. Goal

An investigation notebook reads as the answer first, then the evidence. For
the drawn example, "Why Connected a data source to Built a dashboard
conversion fell" opens with the one-paragraph answer and a "High confidence"
chip, then numbered findings, each with its evidence inline: the segment
breakdown with Postgres in amber; the release row for #4182 with its merge
and deploy times over the daily conversion line and its deploy marker; the
SchemaSyncTimeoutError row with its count, people and "first seen 9 minutes
after the deploy" over daily bars, with a link to Errors; five replay cards,
each "Stuck at 0:42 of 3:10" with what the person did, opening Sessions at
that moment; and the affected accounts with MRR. Then "What I couldn't check",
with a fix button where one exists. The Notebooks list gets an Investigations
group below Personal and Shared, with a status per row (Investigating,
Partial, Complete), and Sessions
can show just the replays an investigation kept.

## 2. What the code does today

- 7.1b (brief, gated, not built) renders notebooks with `NotebookBody`
  (`app/pages/adhoc/notebook/NotebookBody.tsx`, new) from the branch in
  `app/pages/adhoc/sql-dashboard/index.tsx`, gives the header one Re-run
  button (outline, compact) and the actions menu, adds the Notebooks list
  (`app/pages/NotebooksOverview.tsx` with Personal and Shared groups) and the
  "All notebooks" back link.
- 8.1a (brief) stores findings as `finding` cells (`config.finding = {
  investigationId, findingId, kind, title, summary, evidence, data }`), an
  `answer` cell and a `could_not_check` cell, renders them as text cells
  until this unit, and marks the notebook with a root `investigation: { id
  }`. Live steps and findings come from `get-investigation`.
- SQL is shown with `SqlHighlight` (`app/components/SqlHighlight.tsx:275`), as
  3.4a's evidence panel does (`app/components/chat/AnswerEvidence.tsx`).
- Evidence source labels come from `answerEvidenceSourceLabel`
  (`shared/answer-evidence.ts:147`).
- Fix links to a data source use `dataSourceConnectPath`
  (`shared/panel-state.ts:120-122`, `/data-sources?source=<id>`).
- Errors open at `/monitoring?view=errors&issue=<id>`
  (`actions/match-error-issues.ts:17-18`); replays open at
  `/sessions/<id>?atMs=<offset>` (`server/lib/session-replay.ts:2846-2854`,
  read at `app/pages/sessions/SessionDetailPage.tsx:307`).
- The Sessions list builds its query from URL params
  (`app/pages/sessions/SessionsTriagePage.tsx:258-275`) and
  `use-navigation-state.ts` mirrors them (`app/hooks/use-navigation-state.ts:71-86`,
  `sessionFilters` at `:256`). 4.3 (brief) adds funnel params there first.
- `get-session-replay-summary` is access-scoped
  (`actions/get-session-replay-summary.ts:27-40`), so a viewer who cannot
  read a recording gets an error, not its person.

## Plan corrections

- **Next steps belong to 8.2.** The prototype's "Draft emails to affected
  accounts…", "File an issue…" and "Make a deck…" hand work to the agent and
  must produce drafts that need confirmation, which is bet B (8.2). This unit

## 3. Build

1. **Finding cells** `app/pages/adhoc/notebook/FindingCell.tsx`, rendered by
   `NotebookBody` for `chartType: "finding"` and by `SqlChartCard` when one
   sits on a grid dashboard (replacing 8.1a's text fallback). A cell header
   has the finding's number in a small badge and its title (`h3`). Bodies by
   kind, all numbers and dates through `useFormatters`:
   - `answer`: `ConfidenceChip` (8.1f) or "No change found", then the answer
     paragraph. While running, the answer slot shows "Investigating… step
     <n> of <total>: <step label>" with a progress bar.
   - `segments`: a legend with the two windows (previous lighter, current
     solid), one row per value: label, two horizontal bars with their values,
     and the change ("-43.6 pts" or "+277"). The explaining value's current
     bar is amber when it fell. The note line below, muted. Built as a list
     with `role="list"`; the two series use the app's chart color tokens
     (as `SqlChart.tsx` does), never raw colors (`guard:no-raw-colors`).
   - `release`: a row with `IconGitPullRequest`, "#<n> <title>" in medium
     weight, then "<author> merged <date> at <time> · deployed to production
     at <time>" (or "deploy time not recorded"), and a GitHub button (outline,
     xs, `IconExternalLink`) opening the PR in a new tab. Below, the change's
     daily series as a line with the alert threshold dashed when there is
     one, a vertical marker labeled "#<n> deployed", and points past the
     threshold in amber. Then "<N> other pull requests merged in the window."
   - `errors`: a row with `IconBug`, the error title in `code`, "<events>
     events · <people> people · first seen <date> at <time>, <N> minutes
     after the deploy", and an Errors button (outline, xs): a router `Link`
     to `/monitoring?view=errors&issue=<id>` for first-party issues, the
     Sentry permalink in a new tab for Sentry. Daily bars below, amber where
     nonzero.
   - `replays`: a grid of 3 to 5 cards. Each card is a router `Link` to
     `/sessions?investigation=<id>&replay=<recordingId>` (Cmd or Ctrl click
     opens a new tab; read `native-navigation`), with a neutral thumbnail and
     play icon, a timeline bar with the stall marker at `atMs`, "Stuck at
     <m:ss> of <m:ss>", the caption (the agent's, else the marker label) and
     the person, read through `get-session-replay-summary`. A recording the
     viewer cannot read shows "Replay not shared with you" and is not a
     link. `aria-label` "Watch <person> from <m:ss> in Sessions".
   - `accounts`: a table with Account, MRR and People stuck, the top 5 rows
     and a "<N> more accounts" row with their totals. MRR that was not
     recorded reads "Not recorded", never $0; more than one currency shows a
     total per currency.
   - `text`: the paragraph.
   - `could_not_check`: the section "What I couldn't check": one row per
     entry with the amber `IconAlertTriangle`, the step or entry name in
     medium weight, its message, and a fix button (outline, xs) when it has
     one: `connect` links to `dataSourceConnectPath(provider)`, `link` is a
     router `Link`, `agent` calls `sendToAgentChat` with the prompt and opens
     the sidebar.
   - Every finding ends with an evidence row: the source icon and label, the
     window, the rows, and a Query toggle ("Query" / "Hide query") that shows
     the query in `SqlHighlight` for SQL and a plain `pre` otherwise.
2. **Header and meta** (7.1b's notebook header, `index.tsx` branch): on a
   notebook with `investigation`, after the title, a status pill: spinning
   "Investigating…", amber "Partial", "Complete" with an emerald check, or
   "Failed". Actions: while running, Stop (outline, sm, the compact header
   size, `IconPlayerStop`) calling `cancel-investigation`; when finished,
   Re-run (7.1b's button, `start-investigation` with `rerunOf`, tooltip "Run
   the checks again with the latest data"), then the actions menu. Under the
   back link, a meta row: the trigger ("From the alert <name>" linking to the
   alert's dashboard, or "From the panel <title>"), "Ran for <duration> ·
   <spent> of <cap>" ("Running for" while running; "Spend not recorded" when
   unknown), and the sharing state; then one badge per evidence source.
3. **Running view:** while the investigation runs, the owner sees the
   findings so far from `get-investigation` (polled every 2 s while visible)
   in step order, and a skeleton cell titled "<step label>…" for the step
   in progress. Viewers of a shared notebook see the last finished version.
4. **Notebooks list** (`app/pages/NotebooksOverview.tsx`, 7.1b): an
   Investigations group below Personal and Shared, never first, with the
   group badge the other groups have. Rows: `IconZoomQuestion`, the name, a small line "From
   the alert <name> · <date>" or "From <panel> · <date>", and the status pill
   at the right (from `list-investigations`, matched by id). Investigation
   notebooks are not repeated in Personal or Shared. Extend 7.1a's summary
   projection with `config::jsonb -> 'investigation' ->> 'id'` as
   `investigationId` (`server/lib/dashboards-store.ts`, `listDashboardSummaries`).
5. **Sessions filter** (`app/pages/sessions/SessionsTriagePage.tsx`,
   `actions/list-session-recordings.ts`): an `investigation` param maps to a
   new `investigationId` filter ("Only the replays an investigation kept for
   its findings"), resolved server-side through `getInvestigation` access to
   the replay finding's recording ids, then the normal scoped list. A chip
   above the list: `IconZoomQuestion`, "<investigation title>: <stall
   label>", and an `IconX` button "Clear the investigation filter" that
   removes the params. The `replay` param marks that row (`aria-current`)
   and each row's path line reads "Stuck at <m:ss>: <caption>". Opening a row
   goes to `/sessions/<id>?atMs=<atMs>`.
6. **Application state:** `use-navigation-state.ts` publishes
   `investigationId` on an investigation notebook and on Sessions with the
   param; `view-screen` includes it; `navigate` accepts `{ view: "sessions",
   investigationId }` and maps it in `commandPathForNavigation`.
7. **Skills:** in `.agents/skills/investigations/SKILL.md`, how to send people
   to the notebook, the Sessions filter and the fixes.
8. **Changelog:** `agent-native changelog add "Investigation notebooks show each finding with its evidence: the segment, the release, the error spike, replays and affected accounts" --type added`.

## 8. Defaults for open questions

- [Default] Replay thumbnails are a neutral placeholder with a play icon in
  v1, not a captured frame.
- [Default] Investigation notebooks appear only in the Investigations group.
- [Decided by the owner, 2026-10-14] The Investigations group sits below
  Personal and Shared in the Notebooks list, not first. Where a drawn
  screen shows it first, this call wins.
- [Default] Re-run on an investigation notebook re-runs the investigation
  with the same budget; the "Run the accounts check" style fixes start a
  re-run on the extended budget.
- [Default] "No change found" replaces the confidence chip for a no-change
  outcome (not drawn yet).
- [Decided by the owner, 2026-10-14] Phase 8 UI builds only after its
  prototype sign-off; 8.1 is prototyped first.

## 9. Do not

- Do not render Next steps buttons before 8.2's draft-and-confirm flow
  exists; nothing here sends, files or creates anything.
- Do not show a person's email on a replay card the viewer cannot read, and
  do not store it in the cell.
- Do not add a subtitle under the notebook title, an eyebrow, or a count
  strip over the findings.
- No behavior changes outside the unit; additive-only schema; no new /api
  routes for app data (use actions); no raw fetch from client code; no
  process.env reads for provider credentials; no catch or default that turns a
  failure into a normal-looking value; no large payloads in SQL.
