## 1. Goal

Everything a visitor reads before and during sign-in describes Analytics for
founders, operators and growth teams at companies without a data team: one
answer across Stripe, HubSpot and product data, kept as a dashboard, funnel or
alert. The copy stops presenting the app as an Amplitude and FullStory
replacement. This covers the `/home` route meta, the shared sign-in page
marketing for the analytics host (headline, tagline, feature list, meta
description, social card), the CLI template hint, the sign-up verification
email line and the template README. All new copy is a draft for the plan owner
to approve before merge.

README (Markdown, English only):

```markdown
# Analytics

Open source AI analytics for startups and growth teams. Ask a question in
plain language and get one answer across Stripe, HubSpot, product events, and
your database. Keep the answer as a dashboard, a funnel, or an alert. You
don't need a data team or a warehouse.

**Live app: [analytics.agent-native.com](...unchanged...)**

The agent reads your metric definitions, writes the query, joins the tools by
account, and shows its work: the source, time window, filters, and query it
ran. Everything you can do in the UI, the agent can do through the same
actions, and you own the code, the queries, and the data.

## Who it's for

- Founders and operators at 10 to 200 person companies who need numbers from
  Stripe, HubSpot, and the product database without an analyst.
- Product and growth teams who want funnels, retention, and alerts next to
  revenue and CRM data.
- RevOps and GTM teams who need account-level answers across tools.

## Features

- Ask across tools in one question, such as "Which accounts that churned last
  quarter had an open deal in HubSpot?"
- Start on a demo company with Stripe, HubSpot, and product data before you
  connect anything.
- Connect Stripe, HubSpot, PostgreSQL, BigQuery, GA4, PostHog, Mixpanel,
  Amplitude, and more, plus a built-in event collector.
- Dashboards with filters, saved views, funnels, and retention that you edit
  by clicking or by asking.
- Alerts and scheduled email reports.
- Session replay, error tracking, and uptime monitors in the same app.
- A data dictionary the agent follows, so a metric means the same thing in
  every answer.
- Share dashboards per user or per organization with viewer, editor, and
  admin roles.
```

Drop the demo-company bullet if 6.2b has not merged, and drop "your database"
and "PostgreSQL" if 2.1a has not merged.

Translator notes for the locale file: "operators" is not used in any UI

(for example the `sqlDashboard` and alert strings). Do not translate Stripe,
HubSpot or PostgreSQL (`scripts/i18n-no-translate-terms.txt`).
